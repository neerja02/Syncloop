Prototype · health companion for elderly people

A nurse who knows you, calls you, and keeps you on track.

The family sets up a profile once. After that, Asha checks in every day by phone call and WhatsApp, collects BP and sugar readings, reminds medicines, lines up tests before doctor visits, and does a weekly health check. She never prescribes.

Demo walkthrough

  1. Set up the profileTap "Fill sample" to load Ramesh ji, then step through: details, conditions, doctors and medicines, the care plan, and check-in times.
  2. First conversationAsha introduces herself, explains her routine and asks how he feels. Pick a problem, like knee pain.
  3. Daily callUse the call icon. Asha asks in Hinglish, takes the BP reading and marks the morning tablets.
  4. WhatsAppFasting sugar request with a typed reply, then night medicine reminder with one tap.
  5. Next dayAsha remembers yesterday's knee pain and follows up first.
  6. Weekly check and doctor visitA Sunday health check call, and a reminder with the tests and papers to carry.
  7. High readingEnter a high BP. Asha gives calm steps and alerts the daughter.

Asha does

  • Follow up like a family nurse
  • Collect daily, weekly and monthly readings
  • Remind medicines by call or WhatsApp
  • Line up tests before doctor visits
  • Alert family when something is off

Asha never does

  • Prescribe or change medicines
  • Make medical decisions
  • Treat emergencies at home; she says call 112 and alerts family

Sample patients. Calls, WhatsApp and prescription reading are simulated. Asha's voice uses the browser's built-in Hindi voice, which varies by device; Microsoft Edge (Swara) or Chrome (Google Hindi) sound best. The real product would use a natural Indian voice service.
